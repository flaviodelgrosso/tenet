# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in the `loops` project a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, sexual identity or orientation, or other characteristic protected by applicable law.

We welcome contributions that challenge the project's assumptions and improve its reliability. Disagreement about ideas is expected; personal attacks and exclusionary behavior are not.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- being respectful, constructive, and patient;
- assuming good faith while asking for evidence and clarity;
- giving and accepting feedback professionally;
- focusing criticism on ideas, code, documentation, and behavior rather than people;
- taking responsibility for mistakes and helping repair their impact;
- respecting differing experiences, backgrounds, and communication styles.

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or personal attacks;
- sexualized language, imagery, or attention;
- trolling, deliberate disruption, or repeated bad-faith interaction;
- publishing private information without permission;
- threats of violence or encouraging harm;
- unacceptable behavior in any project space or in public spaces when it affects project participation;
- other conduct that would reasonably make participation unsafe or hostile.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may take any action they consider appropriate and proportionate, including removing or editing comments, rejecting contributions, temporarily restricting participation, or permanently banning participation.

Maintainers should apply these standards fairly and consistently, and should protect the privacy of people who report incidents when possible.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in project repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, chats, events, and other spaces when someone is representing the project or when conduct has a clear effect on project participation.

## Reporting

For harassment, discrimination, threats, or other conduct concerns, contact the repository maintainers privately through GitHub. Do not create a public issue for a sensitive report. Include the relevant links, dates, people involved, and any supporting context you can safely share.

For urgent threats to personal safety, contact local emergency services first.

Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as practical. Retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith or participates in an investigation is not acceptable.
